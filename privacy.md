# Psynk Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 22 November 2026
**Publisher:** Michael Kliot
**Contact:** psynksupport@gmail.com

Psynk is a bullet journal app for iPhone. The short version: **your journal stays on your iPhone. We don't collect, see, sell, or share any of your data.**

## What we collect

Nothing. Psynk has no accounts or sign-in, and we don't run any servers. There's no analytics, advertising, tracking, crash reporting, or third-party SDKs in the app. We have no access to anything you write.

## What stays on your device

Everything you put into Psynk is stored only on your iPhone, in the app's local storage, and protected by iOS file protection. That includes entries, tasks, goals, habits, occasions, summaries, the name you enter during setup, and your settings.

- **Widget:** the Psynk widget reads a small snapshot of upcoming items from storage shared only between the app and its widget, on your device.
- **Reminders:** reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. We only ask for notification permission when you set a reminder, and nothing goes through a push server.
- **Preferences:** app settings are stored locally on your device.
- **No cloud sync:** Psynk doesn't sync your data to iCloud or to any other service.

## Backup and export

You can export your journal as a file and import one to restore it. You choose where the file goes, whether that's Files, AirDrop, email, or somewhere else. Psynk doesn't see where you send it. Once the file leaves the app, it's in your hands.

## Holiday import (optional)

If you choose to import public holidays in Settings, Psynk downloads holiday lists over a secure connection from:

- **Nager.Date** ([date.nager.at](https://date.nager.at)). The request includes only the year and the country you chose. See their [privacy policy](https://date.nager.at/legal/privacy).
- **Hebcal** ([hebcal.com](https://www.hebcal.com)), for Jewish and Israeli holidays. The request includes only the year and a fixed Israel location. See their [privacy policy](https://www.hebcal.com/home/about/privacy-policy).

None of your journal content or personal details are sent. As with any internet request, these services can see your device's IP address. If you never use this feature, Psynk makes no such requests.

## Purchases and the free trial

Psynk includes a 7-day free trial, followed by an optional one-time purchase to unlock it for life. There's no subscription and nothing is charged automatically. Apple handles all payments through the App Store, and we never receive your payment or card details. To know when your trial ends, the app reads Apple's signed record of when you first downloaded Psynk and saves that date in your device's secure Keychain. It's used only to check your trial status. Apple's handling of purchases is covered by [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## Deleting your data

You can reset or delete your data inside the app at any time. Deleting Psynk removes its data from your device. Because we never have a copy, there's nothing stored with us to delete.

## Your rights

Data protection laws, including the EU and UK GDPR, the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA), and Israel's Protection of Privacy Law, give you rights to access, correct, delete, and move your personal data, and to object to how it's used. Because Psynk doesn't collect or store any of your personal data on our side, everything is already under your direct control on your device. You can view, edit, export, and delete it yourself. We don't sell or share personal information, as those terms are defined in the CCPA. If you have any questions, contact us at the address above.

## Children

Psynk isn't directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country), and we don't knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.

## Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and the effective date above. If a change is significant, for example if Psynk ever starts collecting any data, we'll make that clear in the app before the change takes effect.
